# Customer Retention & Revenue Analysis (Power BI)

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard focuses on customer retention, revenue
generation, acquisition channels, customer segmentation, geographic
distribution, and monthly revenue performance.
<img width="1121" height="602" alt="CR" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1d340192-c9f9-40d1-9dba-43a073b1dc1d" />

The goal is to understand how effectively the business retains
customers, which acquisition channels generate revenue, and how customer
activity contributes to overall business performance.

## Dashboard KPIs
- Total Customers: 814
- Returning Customers: 724
- Total Revenue: $312K
- Net Revenue After Rejects: $286K
- Customer Retention Rate: 88.9%

## Business Questions
1. How many customers are returning?
2. What is the customer retention rate over time?
3. Which acquisition channels generate the most revenue?
4. How is the customer base distributed by customer type?
5. Which regions have the largest customer populations?
6. How does customer activity change month to month?
7. What is the overall revenue trend?
   
## Key Insights Visible in the Dashboard
- The dashboard reports 814 total customers and 724 returning
  customers.
- The displayed customer retention rate is 88.9%.
- Organic Search is the leading acquisition channel shown,
  generating approximately $69K.
- Paid Social follows at approximately $66K, while Direct
  contributes approximately $62K.
- October shows the highest point on the customer retention trend
  displayed.
- Leather Weekender Bag is highlighted as the top product name.
- Total revenue is $312K, while net revenue after rejects is
  $286K.

Business Value
This dashboard helps decision-makers evaluate customer loyalty,
acquisition performance, regional customer distribution, and revenue
trends. It can support decisions around customer retention,
marketing-channel investment, and revenue growth.
